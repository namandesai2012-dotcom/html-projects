Launch day procedures

  1. Fueling: Technicians pump volatile cryogenic propellants (like liquid oxygen and liquid hydrogen) into the rocket tanks very close to liftoff.
  2. System Checks: Computers and flight controllers test telemetry, electrical systems, and safety mechanisms.
  3. Terminal Countdown: Automated computer systems take over the final minutes to verify engine pressures and internal power before ignition.
